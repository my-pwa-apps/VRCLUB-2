2. Ready for guests?

Without a private DJ invitation, this is only a sound check. In the club, use Music → Live input to test the dancers and lights and choose Go live when you are ready.

You can switch to your DJ software, but do not close this tab or let this computer sleep. Stopping here pauses the feed for every event room; it does not end the event.

Use headphones or hardware monitoring to avoid feedback. Start your music and check both meters before sharing the audience link.

Only perform and broadcast music you are authorized to share. This is a direct audio broadcast, not a Twitch extractor or a recording service.

This private link grants broadcast access. Do not share it with your audience. Its secret is removed from the address bar after loading; keep the original invitation if you need to reopen this page. The event owner can replace it or end the event.

My mixer is not in the list, or there is no sound

Check the cable and the mixer's recording volume. If your interface offers a loopback input, choose that. An ASIO-only output may need a virtual audio cable before a browser can receive it.

## Name and one-liner

**NOCTURNE** — a virtual nightclub for VR headsets, desktop and your friends.

## Short description (about 160 characters)

Step into a WebXR nightclub: a light show that follows your own music, a DJ, a dancing crowd, a bar, a balcony and a street outside.

## Long description

NOCTURNE is a virtual nightclub you can walk around in, on a Quest headset or in a desktop browser.

- **A light show that listens.** Moving heads, lasers, a mirror ball, strobes and a full-wall LED screen follow the beat
  of the music you play. A built-in show director builds from a quiet arrival to a drop and an afterglow, or you can
  take over the lights yourself at the DJ desk.
- **A living room.** A resident DJ at the decks, a dancing crowd that moves in time with the music, a bartender, a
  guest who works the room, a balcony, a lounge and a night street outside.
- **Bring your own music.** There is no built-in music. Play a direct audio link, a file or a folder from your device,
  or a SoundCloud track its creator has published to podcast players.
- **Together.** Optionally join a room of up to eight people with voice, chat, reactions and live hand movement.
  Nothing connects until you choose to.
- **Made for comfort.** Smooth movement or teleport comfort mode, snap turning, and a Photosensitive Safe Mode that
  removes the strobes.

Desktop mode is free. Immersive VR needs access: a one-time purchase (EUR 1.99) or an invitation code. A headset has no
keyboard, so you link it from your phone: the headset shows a short code, you enter it on a web page on your phone,
and the headset starts by itself.

## Content and safety disclosures (use in the age and content questionnaires)

- **Flashing lights.** Strobes and rapid light changes are part of the show. A photosensitivity choice is asked the
  first time the app starts, and Safe Mode can be switched on at any time. State this in the listing.
- **Setting.** A nightclub with a bar. A background character is shown drinking at the bar, and another smokes a
  cigarette outside. No gambling, no violence, no sexual content.
- **User-supplied music.** The app does not ship or rate any music; what plays depends on the links and files the user
  chooses.
- **Online features are optional.** In a room, other people can speak and chat. Hosts can kick, ban and lock rooms and
  anyone can block, mute and mute everyone. Voice uses direct peer connections, so other participants can see your IP
  address; say so in the privacy answers.
- **Locomotion.** Smooth locomotion by default, comfort mode (teleport and snap turn) available at the start.

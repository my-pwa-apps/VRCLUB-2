Use a local audio file, paste a direct HTTPS MP3, M4A, OGG or audio-stream link, or paste a SoundCloud track/set page. Miss Melera Colourizon links use the club's analysed audio stream so the crowd, DJ and lights follow the set; other SoundCloud pages use its official player. YouTube and Spotify page URLs are not playable audio. Direct audio hosts must allow CORS. No music is included.

Other SoundCloud pages play in its official player. Use its controls for position and volume. The crowd and DJ dance while it plays, at an independent tempo, not the track's detected beat. Beat-synced lights, spatial audio and room sharing are unavailable for this source.

Save up to eight sets and choose them from the VR Music menu. Only sets you save here can resume on entry. Links stay on this device; the audio host receives your IP when you play. Share music in a room only when you have permission.

          ⓘ About, Credits & licences

          NOCTURNE WebXR Virtual Nightclub

          NOCTURNE is an immersive social 3D nightclub for WebXR-compatible VR headsets and desktop browsers. Explore a basement club and its city street, join friends online, or take control of the audio-reactive lighting from the VJ desk.

          • Enter in immersive WebXR on a compatible VR headset or explore with keyboard and mouse on desktop.
          • Bring direct music links or local audio files; no music is bundled with the experience.
          • Meet friends in multiplayer rooms with avatars, spatial voice, chat, reactions, and shared music.
          • Control moving lights, lasers, LED visuals, effects, the resident DJ, and the automatic NOCTURNE show.
          • Use Photosensitive Safe Mode and optional VR comfort movement at any time.

          Music
          No music, podcasts or third-party sound recordings are included. Crowd ambience and reverb are generated by the app. Use audio you own or have permission to play and share.
          Links you choose are fetched directly from the audio host, which receives your IP address. Local files stay on your device. In a multiplayer room, the host's music URL and playback position are shared through the relay; guests must choose Listen along before contacting that audio host.
